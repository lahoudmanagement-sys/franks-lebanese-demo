A new website for Frank’s Lebanese Restaurant

Your food has earned 4.3 stars from 662 Google reviews. Your website was built in 2016 and is turning some of those diners away before they reach the door. We have already built the replacement, and you can try it today.

We went through frankslebanesefood.com.au page by page, on a phone and on a computer, in November 2026. Below is what a customer runs into now, and what the new site does instead. The new site is finished and working: https://lahoudmanagement-sys.github.io/franks-lebanese-demo/

What customers run into today

  • The Reservation button leads to “Coming Soon…”

    That page has said “Coming Soon” since June 2016. A booking form does exist, but it sits at the bottom of every page where few people will find it.

    A “Book a table” button is always on screen. The form offers only times you are open, asks for a phone number so you can confirm, and takes under a minute.

  • The opening hours are wrong, and missing on phones

    The site says “Open 9am – 10pm”. Google lists 11 am to 9:30 pm, and 10 pm on Friday and Saturday. On a phone, the hours do not appear at all.

    All seven days are listed, today is highlighted, and the top of the page says “Open now until 9:30 pm” or “Opens tomorrow at 11 am”.

  • The menu is about 25 screens of scrolling on a phone

    There is no way to jump to mains or dessert, and no drink has a price.

    Section buttons stay at the top (Cold mezza, Hot mezza, Chicken, Mains and so on), with a search box and a “Vegetarian only” switch. Prices sit beside each dish.

  • Delivery, takeaway and catering are not mentioned

    You have an Uber Eats store, and your Facebook page offers takeaway and catering. The website links to none of them.

    Four clear choices sit under the main photo: dine in, takeaway, delivery through Uber Eats, and catering.

  • The $55 banquet is easy to miss

    Your best offer for groups is a small block of text halfway down the home page.

    The banquet has its own section with a photo of the full mezza and a button that starts a group booking.

  • Three different opening years

    The home page says “Since 1998”, the About page says 1992, and Facebook says 1993.

    One year, used everywhere. We have used 1992 until you confirm it.

  • Google gets very little to work with

    The site has no page description, no restaurant details in the format Google reads, and 79 of the 80 images on the home page have no description.

    Your address, phone, hours, cuisine and menu are supplied to Google in its own format, and every photo is described.

  • The site is ageing underneath

    It runs on a 2016 WordPress theme with code libraries from 2015, and the administrator’s login name is visible to anyone who looks for it.

    No WordPress, no logins and no plugins to update. There is nothing on the new site to break into.
